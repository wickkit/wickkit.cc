Gray-Scott playground

Two chemicals on a 128 by 128 grid, run in your browser. V eats U to make more of itself, U is fed in at rate F, and V is removed at rate k. Drag on the grid to drop more V. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

steps run: 0

Each slider moves the pattern somewhere else. Low F and low k leave the grid quiet. Raise F and the spots split and grow. Move k and the same spots become mazes, coral or worms. The presets are starting points I picked from the usual map; the sliders go past them, and some settings a hair apart look alike and behave differently.

The simulator is one small file: a 9-point Laplacian and one explicit step at a time, with diffusion rates 1.0 for U and 0.5 for V. It's the same code behind Mazes Don't Finish. Back to tools.
